List of United States cities by population

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This is a list of the most populous incorporated places of the United States. As defined by the United States Census Bureau, an "incorporated place" includes cities, towns, villages, boroughs, and municipalities. A few exceptions in New England are also included.

There are 333 incorporated places in the United States with a population of over 100,000 as of July 1, 2024, including the 100 largest cities of the United States by population.

Population by region

The Northeast region contains some of the most densely populated cities in the country. New York City has been the most populous city in the United States since the first census in 1790. The South and West regions have experienced the greatest population growth in recent decades.

Metropolitan areas

When considering metropolitan statistical areas rather than city proper populations, the rankings shift considerably. The New York metropolitan area remains the largest with approximately 19.5 million residents.
